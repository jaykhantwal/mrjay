1 Video and clip time

Hours work too, like 1:23:45. A stream that is still live stutters when you jump around in it, so use the replay link after the stream has ended. If playback is still choppy, turn off the facecam while you pick times (Layout tab), because it runs a second copy of the video.

2 Best moments finder

How to get a transcript: open the video on YouTube, tap "...more", choose "Show transcript", select all of it and copy it here. You can also load an .srt, .vtt or .txt file. Formats like "0:12 text", "[0:12] text", SRT and VTT all work.
The finder reads the words, not the pictures. It scores each window by excitement, questions, laughter, big statements, numbers, keywords and how strong the opening line is. The score is a ranking against the other windows in this transcript, not a promise of views. Previewing a pick fills in the times and captions, and Auto-edit also picks a caption style, color grade and motion.

YouTube gives us one video, and the streamer's webcam is a small box inside it. The facecam here is a zoomed copy of that box, kept in sync and muted. Captions move up automatically so they don't sit under the facecam.

4 Captions

8 Download clip

  1. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. Phones can't record the screen from a web page.
  2. Press Record. When the browser asks, choose this tab and keep "Share tab audio" ticked, or the file will have no sound.
  3. The preview grows to full height, plays the clip once from start to end, and the file downloads by itself. The recording is cropped to just the vertical frame, with captions, facecam, color and effects included.
  4. Leave the tab open and in front while it records. For "Record all picks", Chrome may ask once to allow multiple downloads.
This records what plays in your own browser, so the quality depends on your computer and the stream. Only clip content you have the right to use, such as your own streams or ones with the streamer's permission. WebM files may not show a total length until they are played, which is normal.
